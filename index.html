RLMM-Flow

Flow Matching Prior Reinforcement Learning for Mobile Manipulation

Shuhang Wang, Ziming Li, Le Zheng, Hui Cheng
Sun Yat-sen University

Paper PDF

Real-world Deployment

Real-world mobile manipulation experiments with RLMM-Flow.

Abstract

RLMM-Flow proposes a point-cloud-conditioned cascaded flow policy and improves a pretrained flow prior through latent-space reinforcement learning. The policy generates base actions before arm actions and performs coarse-to-fine latent steering for high-dimensional mobile manipulation.

RLMM-Flow Pipeline

Overview of RLMM-Flow pipeline.

The framework consists of expert flow pretraining and latent-space RL post-training. A latent critic evaluates generated actions, while coarse-to-fine steering progressively improves the initial noise distribution. The optimized latent variables are decoded by the frozen flow policy into whole-body action chunks.

Network Architecture

Cascaded transformer flow architecture.

RLMM-Flow adopts a cascaded transformer flow policy that explicitly models the heterogeneous structure of mobile manipulators by generating base motion before arm motion. Point-cloud observations provide conditioning information for action generation.
